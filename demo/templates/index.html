What is Rinconada Bikol?

ISO 639-3: bto • Glottolog: irig1242

Rinconada Bikol (also known as Riŋkonāda or Inland Bikol) is an Austronesian language belonging to the Malayo-Polynesian branch, spoken by approximately 385,000 people primarily in the Rinconada district of Camarines Sur, Philippines. It serves as the primary language in Iriga City and the municipalities of Nabua, Baao, Bato, Bula, and Balatan.

Dialectal Variants Sinaranəw (Lakeside/Lowland) and Sinabukid (Highland)
Phonology & Vowels Distinguished by a high central/mid vowel /ə/ or /ɨ/ (often written as e or ə) distinct from Coastal Bikol
Morphological System Highly agglutinative with rich prefixation, infixation, suffixation, circumfixation, and partial/full reduplication
Vitality & Status Classified as stable/safe by the Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino (KWF), but categorized as low-resource in computational linguistics

What is RinTag?

Automated Part-of-Speech Tagger & Morphological Feature Engine

RinTag is a Python library and automated Part-of-Speech (POS) tagger specifically developed by our team for the Rinconada Bikol language. Powered by a Conditional Random Field (CRF) model trained on Rinconada Bikol syntactic and morphological sequential patterns, it is designed to address the digital underrepresentation of regional Philippine languages. RinTag analyzes tokens within context, predicts grammatical categories, computes confidence scores, and outputs morphological feature clues.

Tagset Standard 17 Universal Dependencies (UPOS) tags: ADJ, ADP, ADV, AUX, CCONJ, DET, INTJ, NOUN, NUM, PART, PRON, PROPN, PUNCT, SCONJ, SYM, VERB, X
CRF Probabilities Computes token-level confidence scores and top alternative POS candidate distributions
Affix-Aware Analysis Inspects morphological prefixes (e.g., nag-, naka-, mag-), suffixes, and infixes to disambiguate verbs and nominals
Accessible Evaluation Interactive inspection UI designed for linguists, researchers, and native speakers on both desktop and mobile
